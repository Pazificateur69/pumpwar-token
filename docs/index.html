// Questions

FAQ

Is $PWAR just another memecoin?

No. It's the utility token of PumpWar, a live on-chain arena. It has a working product behind it and real in-app use — boosting, staking, governance — instead of only a chart.

Can the team mint more or freeze my tokens?

No. The supply is fixed at 1B and the mint authority is renounced, so no new tokens can ever be created. The freeze authority is renounced too, so no wallet can be frozen. Both are verifiable on-chain.

Where can I buy it?

Once liquidity is live on mainnet, you'll swap SOL for $PWAR on a Solana DEX (Jupiter / Raydium). The official swap link and contract address will be published here — verify the mint on an explorer first.

What stops the team dumping?

The team allocation (15%) is on a 12-month cliff with on-chain vesting (Streamflow), the treasury sits behind a Squads multisig, and DEX liquidity is locked.

Is this financial advice?

No. $PWAR is a speculative utility token. Do your own research and only risk what you can afford to lose.
